## Brand & Style

This design system powers **ACADEMY SPACE** ("Your Space to Learn & Grow"), a modern academic learning management platform built for modern universities, hybrid academies, and high-impact corporate education.

The visual style blends **Modern Corporate Clarity** with a **Refined Minimalist Editorial** finish. The experience evokes rigorous academic authority balanced by approachable, modern product design. By pairing deep institutional navies with high-energy violet action points, the interface maintains an environment that is focused, distraction-free, and inspiring. 

Key attributes:
- **Professional & Grounded:** Clean structural lines, intentional negative space, and disciplined hierarchical typography.
- **Friendly & Innovative:** Generous card radiuses, vibrant violet calls-to-action, and subtle ambient shadows replace dated, austere academic software patterns.

## Colors

The palette is architected to prioritize content legibility and focal clarity for complex learning pathways, grading workflows, and study spaces.

- **Primary / Brand (`#172554` - Deep Navy):** Anchors headers, persistent navigation, primary milestones, and strong visual hierarchy.
- **Accent / CTA (`#7C3AED` - Violet):** Powers interactive focal points—primary actions, submission buttons, completion triggers, and active step indicators.
- **Soft Accent (`#A78BFA` - Lavender):** Utilized for secondary states, hover halos, soft active highlights, and decorative progress indicators.
- **Canvas & Surface:** Background runs on **Cloud Gray (`#F5F6FA`)**, while active workspaces, modules, and cards use **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**.
- **Typography:** Titles and primary content rely on **Slate (`#1E293B`)**, while metadata, labels, and secondary guidance use **Slate Gray (`#64748B`)**.
- **Structural Outlines:** Subtle divider lines utilize **Border (`#E2E8F0`)**.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Success: `#16A34A` (grades passed, completed modules, published states)
  - Warning: `#D97706` (impending deadlines, review queues)
  - Error: `#DC2626` (submission alerts, overdue assignments, system friction)

## Typography

The type system balances structure and precision:
- **Headings (Manrope):** Geometric, open counters with contemporary proportions. Weights span 600 (Semibold) for section intros up to 800 (Extra Bold) for primary landing titles and course headlines.
- **Body & Data (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral workhorse ideal for long-form curriculum reading, tabular grade books, and dense parameter forms. Standard copy sits at 400 (Regular), with 500 (Medium) and 600 (Semi-Bold) applied for functional labels, table headers, and metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a standard 8pt spatial cadence within a responsive 12-column fluid grid.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and dynamic margins capping at a max content width of 1440px. Sidebars for curriculum modules collapse into an icon tray or drawer at lower widths.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, and 24px side canvas margins.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, and 16px outer safe margins. Dense data tables switch to responsive stacked card formats.

Spacing rhythm prioritizes air around major academic sections while keeping data tables, test forms, and course lists compact and scannable using `space-xs` and `space-sm` internals.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth follows a soft, multi-layered ambient approach tinted directly with the brand's primary deep navy tone (`#172554`) rather than generic black. This keeps elevation natural and cohesive against Cloud Gray backgrounds.

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Base canvas background (`#F5F6FA`) and bordered surfaces.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Multi-layered elevation for cards and actionable content:
  - `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(23, 37, 84, 0.04), 0 4px 16px rgba(23, 37, 84, 0.06);`
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Cards / Popovers):** Elevated feedback state for active course cards and flyout menus:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 8px rgba(23, 37, 84, 0.06), 0 12px 28px rgba(23, 37, 84, 0.08);`
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Used for assignment detail dialogs, grading side-panels, and grade submission drawers:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px rgba(23, 37, 84, 0.08), 0 24px 48px rgba(23, 37, 84, 0.12);`

## Shapes

The design system employs explicit, purposeful radii to establish visual warmth while upholding interface efficiency:

- **Interactive Controls & Inputs (`10px` / `0.625rem`):** Buttons, search bars, text fields, and dropdowns share a consistent 10px radius for unified tactile rhythm.
- **Cards & Containers (`16px` / `1rem`):** Course tiles, assignment cards, grade distribution widgets, and content units utilize a 16px corner radius.
- **Modals & Dialogs (`20px` / `1.25rem`):** Major contextual overlays, quiz modals, and large modal windows use a distinct 20px radius.
- **Pills & Status Badges (`9999px`):** Status tags, academic credit counters, tags, and category chips utilize full circular pill shapes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary / Action:** Background `#7C3AED` (Violet), label Pure White, border radius `10px`. Hover brings `#6D28D9` with subtle ambient shadow. Focus uses a 2px offset halo of Soft Accent (`#A78BFA`).
- **Brand / Institutional:** Background `#172554` (Deep Navy), text Pure White, radius `10px`. Used for high-level LMS actions (e.g., "Publish Course", "Export Gradebook").
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Pure White background, 1px border of `#E2E8F0`, text Slate (`#1E293B`). Radius `10px`. Hover shifts background to `#F8FAFC`.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`), padding `4px 12px`, typography `label-sm`.
- **Active / Enrolled:** `#7C3AED` tint (10% opacity) with `#7C3AED` text.
- **Passed / Completed:** `#16A34A` tint with `#16A34A` text.
- **Pending / Due:** `#D97706` tint with `#D97706` text.
- **Overdue:** `#DC2626` tint with `#DC2626` text.

### Cards & Learning Modules
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border radius `16px`, structural border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, ambient shadow `0 1px 2px rgba(23,37,84,0.04), 0 4px 16px rgba(23,37,84,0.06)`.
- Course progression bars within cards use `#F1F5F9` track with `#7C3AED` fill and a 4px rounded cap.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- Height 44px, radius `10px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#1E293B`.
- Active focus state: border shifts to `#7C3AED` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.15)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size 18px × 18px. Border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Checked state: background `#7C3AED`, border `#7C3AED`, with white check icon. Radius 4px for checkboxes, 50% for radios.

### Modal Dialogs & Drawers
- Radius `20px`, surface `#FFFFFF`, elevation Level 3. Dismiss button positioned top-right with hover wash. Modal backdrop rendered in `#0F172A` with 40% opacity and subtle backdrop blur (4px).

### LMS-Specific Elements
- **Curriculum Tree Nav:** Nested list with 2px vertical guide lines in `#E2E8F0`, active chapter highlighted with `#7C3AED` text and a left-aligned 3px violet pill accent.
- **Grade Progress Rings / Trackers:** Clean track `#E2E8F0` accented by vibrant `#7C3AED` or `#16A34A` metric strokes.
